Why It Cannot Be Replicated

Three Reasons This Asset
Is Truly One of a Kind

The market has changed. Dramatically. What was possible 20 years ago — assembling this much prime land, this close to the coast, with these planning permissions — is no longer possible today. This is not marketing language. It is a fact.

01
Planning Restrictions

Coastal planning laws and environmental protection zones introduced in the region make it legally impossible today to obtain equivalent permissions for a new-build equestrian estate of this size and proximity to the coast.

02
Land Scarcity

77,760 m² of contiguous, titled land in this corridor simply does not come to market. Land of this scale, this close to the main coastal road and the sea, is an extinct asset class.

03
Built Infrastructure Value

The cost to build 19,154 m² of purpose-built equestrian and event infrastructure today would far exceed the asking price — before acquiring the land. The buyer inherits decades of investment at a fraction of replacement cost.

World-Class Infrastructure

Purpose-Built
for Equestrian Excellence

Every facility was purpose-built for excellence — and represents a turnkey platform for the next owner to operate, expand or transform.

The Indoor Arena

24m × 64m arena with state-of-the-art lighting and sound. 1,600 spectator capacity. 1,300 dining guests. Equestrian shows, fashion weeks, gala dinners, product launches, congresses. The centrepiece of the estate.

Outdoor Arenas & Grounds

Professional outdoor dressage and jumping arenas, working paddocks and extensive grounds landscaped with century-old palms. A grand asphalted car park with direct main-road access completes the estate.

Horses & Music Salon

Up to 500 guests (configurable 240–500). Weddings, banquets, corporate launches. Iconic facade with red staircase. A landmark venue in its own right. Pricing on request.

On-Site Restaurant

180-cover licensed restaurant overlooking the arenas. Rustic luxury with fireplace and bar. Available exclusively for private events, weddings and corporate dinners. Pricing on request.

Stabling — 150+ Boxes

Over 150 professional stables for school horses and livery. Full quarantine, tack rooms, wash boxes and veterinary facilities. A fully operational equestrian business from day one.

Residential & Support Buildings

Staff accommodation, offices, café-bar with terrace, veterinary infrastructure, forge and storage. A fully self-sufficient complex — with clear potential for boutique hotel conversion.
